# Comms Audit Deliverable — Outline

Mirrors the ideas42 pattern used for the FAME and Calbright reviews: a durable framework document
plus a partner-facing findings deck.

## Part 1 — Framework (durable, reusable across partners)
1. What ideas42 is; what a behavioral approach means
2. The three stages and twelve principles
3. Per principle: definition · tactics · a do and a don't example · the research behind it

## Part 2 — Findings (partner-specific)
4. Scope, method, and what was audited (comm count by channel, date range)
5. Campaign scorecard — per-comm and campaign means
6. Findings organized **by principle**, one section each, using real partner comms
7. Cross-comm findings — cadence, consistency register, redundancy, journey gaps
8. Prioritized roadmap — quick wins · structural fixes · template proposals
9. Next steps — owner-by-owner sessions

## Appendix
- Every comm audited, grouped by journey phase or owning team
- Redesigned mockups
- Full JSON export

## Notes
- Lead with the framework so recommendations read as evidence-based, not opinion.
- One slide per principle keeps a presentation legible; park detail in the appendix.
- State the audit's limits: what was not in scope, and which timings were unknown.
